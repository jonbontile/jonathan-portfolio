# Project Brief — Bontile Freelance Portfolio

## Purpose and audience
Create a credible portfolio for Jonathan Bontile, CPA, serving prospective freelance bookkeeping/accounting clients and international employers considering remote finance/accounting work. The site should communicate verified capabilities, demonstrate trust through anonymized evidence, and make contact straightforward.

## Positioning
- Brand idea: **Beyond the Numbers**.
- Working process: **Understand → Reconcile → Analyze → Improve**.
- Domain: `portfolio.bontilebiz.com` (ownership, DNS and deployment status pending verification).
- Brand/business styling: Bontile Bookkeeping Services (confirm current/legal styling before publication).
- Voice: clear, calm, precise, human and discreet; explain value plainly without unsupported superlatives.
- Design: hybrid modern corporate/editorial/technology-inspired. Navy and white base, royal orange accent. **Exact orange HEX and source pending verification; do not guess.** Manrope headings and Inter body, subject to license/performance review.

## Outcomes and scope
Visitors should understand who Jonathan serves, review approved work examples, contact him securely, and download a current resume and PDF portfolio. Build a responsive accessible portfolio with Home, About, Services, Case Studies, Contact, Privacy and downloads. Use Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind and Markdown-first content; prefer Cloudflare Workers static assets only after architecture/platform verification.

Contact forms require secure server-side processing, validation, abuse controls and privacy disclosure. Resend is only a candidate after sender/domain and DNS/email verification. Preserve Google Workspace MX records. Keep secrets server-side.

## Sitemap
- `/` Home: positioning, hero, CTA, services snapshot, process, selected approved studies.
- `/about/`, `/services/`, `/case-studies/`, `/case-studies/[slug]/`, `/contact/`, `/privacy/`.
- Resume download and PDF portfolio download (final assets and URLs pending).
- Accessible 404 page.

## Case-study concepts (not claims)
Potential topics, only if Jonathan supplies evidence and approves disclosure: month-end close/reconciliation; bookkeeping cleanup/reporting readiness; management reporting/decision support. Each study must anonymize, state scope and approach, distinguish observed facts from client-reported results, and omit metrics absent documented evidence and permission. No composite presented as a real engagement.

## Constraints and pending facts
Do not fabricate clients, outcomes, testimonials, credentials, employment history or metrics. Do not publish confidential data or unapproved contact details. Pending: verified credential jurisdiction/display style, services, availability, history, tools, contact address, headshot and permission, case-study evidence/consent, resume/PDF, exact orange/logo, privacy retention, domain/deployment state and adapter/runtime.

## Verification gates
1. Inspect repository and deployment architecture before choosing implementation details.
2. Confirm the orange from an identified brand source; record source and HEX or leave pending.
3. Verify every professional claim and obtain case-study/asset approval.
4. Review existing Google Workspace DNS and mail flow before any email-related setup; preserve MX.
5. Approve privacy, content, contact flow and launch before publication.

## Acceptance criteria
Both audiences have clear next steps; every public claim is sourced and approved; mobile hero puts text/CTA before genuine headshot; keyboard access, semantics, focus, contrast, alt text, reduced motion and responsive behavior are addressed; contact is secure and server-side; downloads are current and accessible; Markdown remains the content source of truth.
