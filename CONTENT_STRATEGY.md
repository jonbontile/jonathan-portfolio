# Content Strategy

## Audiences and message
Address freelance bookkeeping/accounting clients and international employers with clear, distinct paths. The central idea is **Beyond the Numbers** and the working method is **Understand → Reconcile → Analyze → Improve**. Explain how reliable books and useful reporting support decisions without promising a financial result.

## Voice
Professional, approachable, careful and discreet. Prefer plain language, explain necessary accounting terms, be specific about scope, and avoid inflated claims or unsupported guarantees.

## Page roles
- Home: verified value proposition, audience paths, CTA, process, approved studies.
- About: verified background, credentials, approach and confirmed tools.
- Services: deliverables, boundaries, fit and inquiry CTA.
- Case studies: anonymized evidence and methodology.
- Contact: minimal secure form and verified alternative contact method; response timing only if confirmed.
- Resume/PDF portfolio: current owner-approved files with accessible filenames.
- Privacy: data, purpose, processors, retention, contact and rights as applicable; obtain appropriate review.

## Case-study template
Title without identifying client details; generalized context/challenge; scope and constraints; approach (map to the four-step method only when accurate); outcome only if documented and approved; label client-reported outcomes; omit unsupported metrics; lessons/limitations; disclosure of anonymization. Never present a composite or hypothetical as a real engagement.

Possible evidence-gathering topics (not assertions of prior work): month-end close/reconciliation, bookkeeping cleanup/reporting readiness, management reporting/decision support.

## Search and media
Use descriptive metadata based on verified services. Avoid keyword stuffing, unsupported geography/service claims and fabricated structured data. Add canonical/social metadata only after domain verification. Use meaningful link text and alt text; decorative visuals get empty alt text.

## Workflow
Markdown is source of truth. Draft → source/fact-check → privacy/anonymization review → owner approval → publish. Keep a private claim ledger where practical. Do not place confidential client files, personal data or unapproved testimonials in the repository.

## Pending inputs
Credential jurisdiction/display style, services, history, tools, availability, location/time-zone or work authorization wording, contact and response time, case-study sources/consent, resume/PDF, headshot/permission, privacy/retention and domain verification.
