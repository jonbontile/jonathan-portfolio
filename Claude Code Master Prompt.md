## 1. Project Context

We are developing a professional portfolio website for Jonathan Bontile, a Philippine Certified Public Accountant and founder of Bontile Bookkeeping Services.

The website will serve two primary audiences:

1. International freelance clients looking for bookkeeping, accounting, financial reporting, and financial analysis services.
2. International employers and recruiters looking for qualified remote accounting and finance professionals.

The portfolio must establish Jonathan's individual professional identity while maintaining a connection to Bontile Bookkeeping Services.

The website is a complementary professional platform, not a replacement for the existing business website.
