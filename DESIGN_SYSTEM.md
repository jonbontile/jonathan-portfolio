# Design System

## Direction
Hybrid modern corporate, editorial and technology-inspired: rigorous yet human, with strong hierarchy, whitespace, restrained data-inspired motifs and genuine photography.

## Color
Navy and white/off-white are the base; royal orange is the accent. Exact Bontile orange HEX is **pending confirmation** from an identified source. Do not guess or claim a temporary value is official. Centralize semantic tokens for text, surfaces, borders, accent, focus, success, warning and error; check contrast for each pairing.

## Type
Manrope headings and Inter body/UI are the agreed direction, pending licensing and performance review. Use appropriate fallbacks and loading that avoids layout shift. Keep line lengths readable and scales responsive.

## Layout and components
Desktop home hero: split content and portrait. Mobile: message and CTA before genuine headshot. Use consistent spacing/grid and comfortable reading widths. Shared parts: accessible header/nav, skip link, buttons, cards, process steps, case study, labeled form/errors, downloads and footer. Links must remain identifiable without color alone.

## Motion and accessibility
Subtle purposeful feedback only. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; no content depends on animation. Avoid parallax, autoplay and flashing. Use semantic landmarks, logical headings, keyboard operation, visible focus, usable targets, labeled forms and announced errors, alt text, sufficient contrast and zoom/reflow support. Hide decorative graphics from assistive technology or provide text equivalents.

## Assets and responsive review
Use only owner-approved logo, genuine headshot, resume and portfolio files. Verify rights/crops; do not invent client marks, screenshots, dashboards or testimonials. Review narrow phones, phones, tablets and desktop, including navigation, hero order, long headings, focus, form and download states.
