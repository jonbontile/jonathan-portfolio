# Visual Design Brief

## Objective
Present Jonathan Bontile, CPA, as a careful finance professional whose work goes **Beyond the Numbers**. Balance corporate trust, editorial clarity and subtle technology cues for freelance clients and international employers.

## Composition
- Split-screen home hero with concise value statement, primary CTA and genuine headshot.
- On mobile, text and CTA come first; portrait follows with intentional crop.
- Lead visitors through positioning, audience/service routes, process, approved work and contact.
- Use editorial type hierarchy, generous whitespace, structured grids and restrained data-inspired accents.

## Palette and type
Navy/white base, royal-orange accent. Exact Bontile orange HEX and its source are **pending verification**; do not approximate as final brand color. Manrope headings and Inter body are the agreed direction, subject to license/performance confirmation.

## Imagery and interaction
Use Jonathan's genuine approved headshot; none was supplied with this brief. Do not substitute generated or stock portraits. Diagrams must be truthful; no fictional dashboards or artifacts implying real results. Use calm hover/focus/reveal details, keep content available statically, honor reduced motion, and make the CTA keyboard accessible.

## Avoid
Generic finance stock imagery, fake client logos, fabricated testimonials/results, unreadable chart decoration, crowded layouts, unverified orange, motion-heavy effects, or mobile layouts that place the portrait before the message.

## Review
Record orange source/HEX or leave pending; confirm headshot/permission; check desktop split and mobile order; review contrast, keyboard, focus, semantics and reduced motion; approve/anonymize case studies; confirm current accessible downloads.
